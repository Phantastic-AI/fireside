# Changelog

The commit trail tells this story in full; these are the stations.

## 1.0 — the polish (2026-08-12)

The reseed door got a real lock, past conferences learned to speak in the
past tense, and every screen was walked by every kind of person it was
built for: an organizer released six hundred and eleven decisions in one
confirmed act, a reviewer's scores stayed private until submitted and then
locked, a speaker's portal kept quiet about letters that had not left, and
a stranger signed up cold and started a conference.

## 0.4 — the last faces (2026-08-12)

Sharing arrived, opt-in to the bone: star a talk, choose to share, see who
else is going, change your mind and be gone that instant. The schedule
stopped pretending physics away — overlapping stars and tight room-to-room
turns say so. The same doors opened for agents at `/mcp`. Ask began
answering with doors instead of paragraphs. Calendar files for everything.
Proposals became editable until the call closes, older words kept.

## 0.3 — the backstage (2026-08-12)

Nine rooms behind one door: the events hall, the pile with honest counts,
one proposal whole, the outbox where telling is a deliberate act, the
agenda builder that refuses conflicts at placement, people, the green room
with its revocable share link, slides, settings with the question editor
and the team, and the reviewer's room. Deciding stages; releasing sends;
the number a person confirms is the number that goes.

## 0.2 — the doors (2026-08-12)

The public faces: an event home, the call for speakers with teaching
placeholders and autosave, the speaker portal that never leaks an unsent
decision, the agenda on one bar of venue wifi, the speaker gallery, and an
anonymous my-schedule. Three ways to sign in.

## 0.1 — the spine (2026-08-11)

One Worker, one database, twenty tables, and the rules that make the rest
safe to build: a state machine that refuses illegal moves in the database
itself, guard statements that abort a write when the world moved, and a
deterministic demonstration world — two conferences, a thousand proposals,
six hundred and ten decisions made and not yet told — rebuilt to the same
instant on demand.
